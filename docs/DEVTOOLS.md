# Gridfall dev tools

Console helpers for the dev server (`npm run dev`). None of them ship in a production build. Moved from the README in 10.0.

- `await __game.checkRoutes()` runs:
  - player routes on all three layers;
  - vaults and drops;
  - nav-graph connectivity;
  - guard chases from every door.
- `__game.debug()`: FPS, draw calls, lights, alert level, civilian states, music layers.
- `await __dev.vision()`: 24 perception scenarios on the real map, including:
  - behind or beside a guard, out of range;
  - walls, a roof right above a guard, the station under the street, walkways in clear view;
  - the guard turning, holstered vs drawn.
- `await __dev.pursuit()`: headless pursuit scenarios with the real AI at 30 Hz.
  - Covers escapes by roof, alley and metro, hiding close by, a shot during the search, and re-detection.
  - Reports the response size, the time to calm, whether you were found, and whether any guard ever went for your hiding spot without information.
- `await __dev.ai(true)`: world overlay with view cones, lines of sight, guard states and suspicion, nav goals, search points and the last known position.
- `await __dev.perf(seconds)`: frame rate, CPU and GPU milliseconds per frame (GPU timer queries), busy share of each second.
- `await __dev.story({ watchIntro: true })`: plays Story Escape like a player who only reads the screen.
  - It steers the real controller with real keys along the route the game shows.
  - It presses E and holds E at the prompts, and fights or hides when hunted.
  - It boards the express and logs every objective and subtitle.
- Settings (3.4):
  - `__dev.start('arcade', 'desert')` loads a setting and plays immediately. `__game.loadLevel('tokyo')` switches settings.
  - `await __dev.levelAI({ spawns: 24, sight: 25 })` works on any level: the nav graph from every spawn, runners to every test goal (inside every building, every roof), and gunners must get a shot off within the given sight range.
  - `__game.debug().memory` lists GPU geometries and textures. After switching back and forth, they must return to the same numbers.
  - `__game.level.storm.clock.set('peak', 1)` (desert) forces the storm.
  - Checkpoints in Tokyo-only tools are `game.tokyo.*`; they throw if Tokyo isn't loaded.
- World integrity (3.3), which in 3.4 works on any level. The reasoning and results are in `docs/WORLD-VALIDATION-3.3.md`:
  - `await __dev.world()`: scans the whole map on a 0.5 m grid with the real collision and finds escapes, track-pit access, traps, areas outside the designed zones, invisible walls, visuals you can walk through and gaps you can't fit through.
  - `await __dev.stress({ samples, fps })`: the real controller from sampled reachable cells in 8 directions, walking and sprint-jumping. `fps` takes rates and `'spiky'`, for example `[20, 30, 60, 120, 'spiky']`.
  - `await __dev.train()`: boarding at every door, closed doors, riding a departing car, and someone standing in a doorway as the doors close (with the real rail).
  - `await __dev.colliders(true)`: the collision overlay around the camera.
  - `await __dev.bench(3)`: fixed benchmark scenarios for the loaded setting (Tokyo, the desert or the jungle). Compare the results with `docs/BENCH-3.2.json` … `docs/BENCH-4.2.json`. A measurement only counts if the window stayed focused, visible and in play (`valid`); an unfocused window or Chrome's Energy Saver caps the frame rate.
  - `await __dev.bench(3, { pipelines: ['v4', 'raw', 'v37'] })` (4.0): every scenario in each render pipeline, interleaved.
  - `await __dev.sheet(['yard', 'tower'], ['v37', 'v4'])` (4.2, jungle views): a contact sheet of frozen frames in an overlay (click to close). `await __dev.hdr(names, hide?)`: linear HDR luminance percentiles per view, or, with `hide`, of the pixels those objects add.
  - **F8** (dev server only) cycles the render pipeline: 4.0 → RAW (no composer, the same materials) → 3.7 (the old look and materials) → 4.0. In the desert it switches the 4.1 additions (sun, grade, glare, glints, metals, haze) the same way. `__dev.pipeline('v37')` does the same from the console.
  - `__game.safety.recoveries`: how often the out-of-bounds safety net fired. It should stay 0, and each recovery is logged as `[safety]`.
- Survival tactics (5.0):
  - `await __dev.tactical()`: headless scenarios on the loaded setting: roles (suppressors, flank sides, shot tokens, rushes), the same with a moving player, flanking a gunner's cover (time until it relocates), suppression (pinned, silent, resumes), losing the squad (hunt, search, never his live position), the desert storm, and a 120 s soak (bounds, inside geometry, stalls, cost).
  - `await __dev.tactics(true)`: overlay with roles, actions, cover lines (green while the cover hides the hunkered body from you, red once blown), peek spots, rush slots, the LKP and the search points.
  - `await __dev.survival({ setting, seconds, startWave })`: a bot plays Arcade Survival (moves, aims, fires); reports kills, damage events, enemy shots, action shares, callouts and integrity.
  - `await __dev.aiBench()`: 10 enemies per setting; AI milliseconds per frame (average, P95, P99, worst) and rays per frame. Compare with `docs/BENCH-5.0.json`.
- Team Deathmatch (6.0), on the desert:
  - `await __dev.tdmSoak({ seconds: 300, timeScale: 2 })`: the bots play alone (you spectate in god mode, tickets 999): kills per minute, ranges, flank kills, what victims were doing, stuck bots, friendly-fire counters, scheduler pressure, AI cost.
  - `await __dev.tdmBench({ withPlayer: true })`: frame cost at 1x with all nine bots fighting (AI ms, rays, decides per frame, FPS, CPU, GPU).
  - `await __dev.friendlyFire()`: rounds through teammates (each team), your shots through allies, a teammate's blade, check-fire, rounds at you; `ok: true` when nothing hurts a teammate. Compare with `docs/BENCH-6.0.json`.
- ADS and crouch (11.0):
  - `await __dev.world({ bodyHeight: 1.2 })` scans with a crouched body (compare `perZone` with a standing scan to find crouch-only spaces).
  - `await __dev.stress({ crouch: true })` starts from every crouched-reachable cell and runs crouch-walks, and crouch-then-sprint-jumps (standing up under low ceilings included).
  - `__game.player.ads` / `.crouch` / `.height` read the blends; `__dev.colliders(true)` draws the head ring at the current height.
- Arena aiming (11.0):
  - `tdmSim` reports `arena.aim`: aimed vs hip rounds, the hit rate of each per range band (`hitPctByRange`; every round carries its own mode and range), and the share of time aiming.
  - `tdmSim({ ads: false })` is the 10.0 shooter for an A/B.
  - Each gunner exposes `isAiming` and `aimRaise`.
- Mobility, spotting, Desert 2.0 (12.0):
  - `__game.player.sliding` / `.slideV` / `.slideK` read the slide.
  - `tdmSim` reports `arena.flank` (started, arrived, engaged from the flank, kills), `arena.retreat` (by why, lived, died retreating) and `arena.slides`. `tdmSim({ v12: false })` is the 11.0 bot for an A/B.
  - `tdmBench({ withPlayer: true, spotting: false })` turns the player's spotting and the minimap off; the result has `minimapMs` and `sensingWindow`.
  - `TDM.spotting` switches the spotting off in play.
- Loadouts and map transitions (13.0):
  - `tdmSim({ arms: 'random' | 'smg' | 'ar' | 'shotgun' })`: the bots' guns (`'random'`, the default, is the armory deal with at most two of one gun on a team; one id arms everyone). The result has `byWeapon`: kills and the kill-range median / p10 / p90 for each gun. `ads: false` now arms everyone with a rifle that never aims.
  - `await __dev.mapCycle({ maps, laps, play })`: TDM through the result screen's real next-map cards round the maps (default desert → docks → jungle, two laps). Per map it records geometries, textures, programs, scene objects, DOM nodes, minimaps, name tags, the heap and the build time, and compares lap 2 with lap 1. Its WeakRef check can't force a GC, so a level it lists as alive may only be uncollected yet.
  - `node scripts/leakcheck.mjs [laps] [maps]` (Node 22+, Google Chrome; `URL=` to point at another server): the definitive check. Headless Chrome over the DevTools protocol does the same loop, forces a full GC at every map (heap per map), tags everything each left level built, and prints the strong retainer path of anything still alive from a heap snapshot. Exit code 1 if anything leaked. Run it against a server nobody is editing (hot reload restarts the page).
  - `__game.loadout` (`current`, `queued`), `__game.playerName`, `__game.tdm.slots[i].arm`.
- Tokyo: Neon Block, skins and vertical AI (14.0):
  - `?setting=neonblock`. `bench(3)` has a Neon Block set: nine views and a firefight, with the match running.
  - `tdmSim` reports `vertical`:
    - bot time on the street, the mid level (2.5–6 m) and the top (> 6 m);
    - drop-downs taken, and where from;
    - kills by the killer's floor.

    It also reports `arena.flank.high` (flank spots above the contact). `tdmSim({ vantage: false })` is the 13.0 flank for an A/B.
  - `TDM.skins = 'legacy'` brings back the 6.0–13.0 looks (garrison vs the level's survival enemies) for side-by-side screenshots.
  - `__dev.world()` now counts instanced props as drawn (per instance), and skips per-frame instanced meshes (rounds, brass).
- Arena flow (15.0):
  - `tdmSim` reports `laneTimePct` (bot time in each of the map's three lanes, the contested middle only) and `killsByLane` (by where the victim died).
    - Lanes come from `TdmData.laneOf`. Docks: containers, warehouse, waterfront. Crash Site: ridge, wreck, ravine. Neon Block: west, centre, east.
    - `arena.laneMoves` counts bots moved off a hot lane.
  - `tdmSim({ pressure: false })`: lanes by head count alone (14.0), for an A/B.
  - `bench(3)` has a Crash Site set (eight views and a firefight) and three new Docks views (quay gantry, crane nest, container gantry).
- Old Port (17.1):
  - `?setting=oldport`. `bench(3)` has an Old Port set: nine views (the hall, the upper floor in the skylight shafts, the yard, the stacks, the dock floor, the bridge, the sea quay into the sun side, the whole port) and a firefight. `tdmSim` lanes: warehouse, yard, dock.
  - `__game.level.debug()`: nav nodes and drops, skylight shafts, indoors, `drowned` (the sea fence should keep it 0).
- Fog and music (25.0):
  - `tdmSim(...).fog` (a map with a ground fog): kills on the ground (both ends under its top), on the roofs (both over) and across, with medians and p10/p90; each gun's share of its time over the fog; the sight checks the fog cut. `tdmSim({ fog: false })` plays the map without the fog's sight cap and route bias.
  - `await __dev.musicSpectrum({ ids, seconds, intensity })`: each procedural theme rendered offline (all layers in at `intensity`, mixed as the game mixes it), and again through a high-pass at 1 kHz and 2.5 kHz: the share of the energy above each, the RMS and the peak (before the limiter).
- Stamina (30.0):
  - `tdmSim(...)` reports `stamina`: sprint time (share of the bots' alive time) and its reasons (crossing, flanking, pushing, retreating), the time run dry, pools emptied a bot-minute, and the pool when a fight starts (median, p10, the share under 20). `stamina: false` is 29.0's endless sprint (the A/B).
  - `STAMINA` and `WEIGHT` (`src/combat/Stamina.ts`) are the knobs; `ARENA.stamina` the bots' reserve and floors.
- Last stand (29.0):
  - `tdmSim(...)` reports `endgame`: the outcome and how (elimination, sudden death, trade, sudden death draw), when the first team ran out of tickets and who stood then, the endgame's length, a comeback, the final standing, the sudden-death clocks (starts, resets, the longest run), and last-stand bots against the same bots before (nearest teammate, crouched, moving, holding). `lastStand: false` turns the last-stand instincts off (the A/B); the tickets default to the map's (50).
  - `__game.tdm.rules`: the match rules (tickets, the clocks, the outcome); `__game.tdm.standing()`; `__game.tdm.debug()` includes them.
- Flanks (28.0):
  - `tdmSim(...)` also reports `edge` (the share of bot time, combat time and kills on the map's outer edges, `TdmData.edgeOf`) and `xBands` (bot time, combat time and kills in 12 bands across the map's width), and takes `sight` (a fixed sight range for the match, the fog density with it: the desert's storm doesn't run in a sim; 24.5 is its peak).
  - `bench(3)` gains views of the new pieces: the depot, the sniper stack and the barges (docks); the trench, the Anvil's top and the convoy (desert, calm).
- Deep Jungle overhaul (33.0):
  - `tdmSim(...)` reports `wallPress` (a bot running over 1.5 m/s while covering under 40% of it for 1.5 s+: events, seconds, a bot-minute, the worst spots by state) and `scrape` (moving bots within 8 cm of something solid, by zone). `corridor: false` turns the corridor feelers off (`ARENA.corridor`, on through Deep Jungle's profile); `directRise` now applies after the map's profile (it was overwritten).
  - `__dev.foliageSight({ n })`: sightlines between random standable spots blocked by plants at standing (eye 1.6 → chest 1.3) and crouched height, spots with a plant over eye height within 2.5 m, low vines, every plant kind's count, triangles and tallest.
- Night Tokyo overhaul (34.0):
  - `await __dev.fog()`: the live HeightFog module (`{ at, on }`): `at(from, to)` is the hidden share between two points as the picture draws it (the bots' sight uses the same function); a page `import()` of the module gets a second instance with the fog off, so tests in the page go through this.
  - `tdmSim(...).fog` now splits ground / roof / across by the fog's dense top (Neon Block 6.2 m: the landings and the footbridge count as ground).
  - `bench(3)` on Neon Block gains four views: the Lantern Bridge, the Transit Rail's deck, the Lantern Market, a roof looking down into the smog.
  - `Nav.stats.nearestEntries`: routes entered by the nearest-node fallback. `FoliageSite.plants`: a site's plant sizes and counts. `world/Containers`: the shared container (`addContainer`, `overgrownContainerTexture`).
- Neon Docks overhaul (32.0):
  - `tdmSim(...)` reports `spread`: bots with 2+ teammates within 10 m (`groupedPct`), one within 6 m (`pairedPct`), in a convoy (2+ within 15 m moving the same way, `convoyPct`), each by state (`groupedBy`, `convoyBy`); the nearest teammate (median, p25); the share of the main lanes a team covers (`laneCoverPct`) and its width (`widthPct`); hunts sent to a side approach. Teams of 3+ alive with tickets left, every 15 steps. `space: false` is 31.0's bots (the A/B; `ARENA.space` the knobs, off by default and on through the docks' profile, `DOCKS_AI`).
  - `TDM.force.size` / `tdmSim({ size })` as before; the docks' own size is 9 (`DOCKS_TEAM_SIZE`).
  - `Rain` takes a `RainLook` (streaks, width, length, opacity, glow, near fade, splashes); `DOCKS_RAIN` is the docks'.
- Deployment zones (31.0):
  - `TDM.spawns`: `'zones'` (the default: 2–3 zones a team, `TdmData.zones`), `'dynamic'` (27.0's map-wide pads, DEV only now), `'bases'` (the yards). `tdmSim({ spawns })` takes all three; `spawnSafety.mode` names the one that ran.
  - `tdmSim(...)` reports `zones` (picks, zone-safety rays, picks with no safe zone, uses per `team-zone`) and `zBands` (bot time, combat time and kills in 12 bands from one side's back to the other's).
  - `__dev.zoneReport()` (a TDM match running): each zone's team, letter and centre, its spawn points (validated at setup), the nearest enemy zone, the enemy zones that see it (centre eye to centre chest), and whether the nav reaches it. `__dev.spawnPadPlan(px)` draws the zones too.
- Dynamic spawns (27.0):
  - `TDM.spawns = 'bases'` (DEV): a map with pads plays from its yards and 19.0 forward points again (the A/B).
  - `await __dev.tdmSim({ spawns: 'dynamic' | 'bases', cell: 8 })`: also reports `spawnSafety` (respawns; died within 3 s and 5 s; shot within 3 s; time to death and to first hit; the nearest hostile at spawn; fallbacks; rays and ms per pick; uses per pad) and `flow` (an occupancy grid of `cell` m over the walkable cells: coverage, the normalized entropy of bot time, the Gini of kills over cells, the busiest tenth's share of kills), and `patrol` (picks, to an active area, heard spawns, arrivals).
  - `__dev.spawnPads({ n, zMin, keep, seed })`: pad proposals for the loaded map (nav nodes in a lane with room for a pad; farthest-point from the kept ones, then Lloyd relaxation, sheltered spots first), as `[x, y, z, yaw, exposure]`.
  - `__dev.spawnPadReport()` (a TDM match running): each pad's side, exposure, the pads it sees, its nearest pad, room and reach. `__dev.spawnPadPlan(px)`: a top-down PNG (data URL) of the map, its pads and every pad-to-pad sightline.
- HUD and reload audio (26.0):
  - `node scripts/boardcost.mjs [kills=200]` (Node 22+, Google Chrome): headless, a TDM match with the loop stopped; each step is a kill on the team board and a frame. It prints JS, style and layout time per kill, and the rows' final order.
  - `node scripts/reloadcheck.mjs`: every gun's reload stages rendered offline (48 kHz, dry): length to −40 dB, peak, spectral centroid and the energy share under 250 Hz, with the pre-26.0 reload as a reference row. Also the live engine's main-thread cost per `reload()` call. `LEAK=1` renders the noise layers as the engine played them before 26.0 (started at once).
  - `uicheck.mjs` checks overlaps among the team board, the score and the feed too (instead of the 24.0 scoreboard column).
- UI checks (24.0):
  - `node scripts/uicheck.mjs [out-dir]` (Node 22+, Google Chrome; `URL=`, `SIZES=1440x900,3840x2160`): headless Chrome at 1440×900, 1920×1080, 2560×1440 and 3840×2160. It checks the menu, the maps screen, a TDM HUD (a kill of the player's in the feed) and the result screen: the smallest visible text, anything off screen, overlaps among the minimap, health, callsign, ammo and live scoreboard. Screenshots go to `out-dir`. Exit 1 on a spill, an overlap or text under 11 px.
  - `node scripts/csscheck.mjs [seconds]` also measures a hovered button, a hovered map card (a hover must settle) and the TDM HUD after a kill, and stops the logo's glitch for the hover rows.
- Old Port overhaul (23.0):
  - `await __dev.sightlines({ band: [x0, x1] })`: lists the spawn-side to spawn-side lines whose middle lies in that x band (up to 40), to find what carries them.
  - `tdmSim({ directRise })`: the arena bots' straight-walk limit for this run (Infinity is the 22.0 bot; Old Port's profile sets 1).
  - `__game.spawnZone` (`open`, `at`): the player's spawn area for the weapon swap.
- Character rigs (22.0):
  - `await __dev.rigs({ raise, pitch, yaw, kit, team, dist })`: lines up four merc rigs in front of him, one per gun, posed with the bots' own hold and IK (`raise` 0 the hip, 1 aimed). `__rigs()` removes them. For checking the grips.
  - `await __dev.ik(n)`: IK solves so far, rigs built (and how many merged geometry), and the cost of one pose plus two solves (µs).
- Weapons (21.0):
  - `await __dev.gun(id, theme?)`: puts `'pistol' | 'smg' | 'ar' | 'shotgun'` in his hands, in a finish (`'blaster' | 'military' | 'corporate'`; default: the map's). Returns the finish, what it fires and its sound.
  - `await __dev.gunCycle(laps)`: every gun in every finish, a frame rendered each; GPU geometries, textures and programs after each lap (flat: the swaps free what they build).
  - `tdmSim({ arms: 'pistol' })` arms every bot with one gun; `byWeapon` tags are `PST`, `SMG`, `AR`, `SG`.
- Maglev overhaul (20.0):
  - `await __dev.sightlines({ front, minDist, pairs })`: eye-height lines between the nav graph's walkable nodes: `spawnToSpawn` (every node beyond `front` on one side to every one on the other: clear share, longest, the x bands that carry them) and `longPct` (random pairs `minDist`+ m apart, by band).
  - `await __dev.camo({ kit, setting })`: starts a TDM match with that operator kit and poses an OMEGA bot at 12/20/30 m in fixed views, rendering each with and without it: on-screen pixels, brightness contrast, colour difference.
  - `await __dev.sniperDrill({ seconds, at, size, profile })`: a live match (999 tickets); the player, in god mode, is a perfect marksman at `at` firing at OMEGA bots 20–70 m away. Kills a minute, the share of bots hit that got out of sight alive, exposure times. Real time: don't edit `src/` while it runs (a hot reload kills it).
  - `tdmSim(...).difficulty`: lives hit from 22 m+, the share that lived 3 s more, time from a life's first hit to its death, kills beyond 25 m, evasions (`ARENA.underFire`) and evasion slides.
- Self-play density (19.0):
  - `tdmSim({ size, tickets, profile, forward })`: `size` players a team (bot vs bot: the player's slot is filled, so NvN; until 18.0 the sims ran (N-1)vN), `profile: false` turns the map's AI tuning off, `forward: false` the forward respawns. New in the result: `teamSize`, `firstEngagement` (median, p90, mean of each life's spawn-to-first-round time; lives that died without firing), `quietPct` (seconds nobody fired), `longestQuiet`, `forwardSpawns` (forward / yard respawns).
  - `tdmBench({ size })`: the frame cost with that many a side (the player in the match: `withPlayer`).
  - `bench(3)` on Crash Site: the temple's front, the undercroft, inside the hull, its roof, the shrine.
- Maglev Terminal (18.0):
  - `?setting=maglev`. `bench(3)` has a Maglev set: nine views (the train's aisle, platform 1 to the far end, the track bed, the mezzanine, the bridge, looking up through the glass roof, along the curtain wall, the whole hall from the balcony) and a firefight. `tdmSim` lanes: west, train, east; the zones name each car (CAR 1–3, GANGWAY).
  - `__game.level.setVisual(false)` hides the glass (for an A/B of its cost with `perf()`); `__game.level.debug()`: nav nodes, hand links, drops, point lights.
  - The central-pull settings can be switched off for an A/B: set `laneWeights` and `laneStrict` on `__game.level.data.tdm` to `undefined`, set `__game.nav.cost = null`, then clear `__game.nav.fields` and `__game.nav.vaultFields`.
- UI flavor and the menu stage (16.0):
  - `node scripts/stagecheck.mjs [cycles=6]` (Node 22+, Google Chrome; `URL=` for another server): drives the real menu (root → a mode's maps → BACK, with gun swaps) in headless Chrome. It tags each menu stage and every swapped gun, forces a full GC and checks a heap snapshot for survivors (exit 1 on any). It also asserts that the stage is gone on the maps screen and after DEPLOY. Shares `scripts/lib/cdp.mjs` and `scripts/lib/heap.mjs` with `leakcheck.mjs`.
  - `__game.stage` (null except on the menu's root and controls screens), `__game.syncStage()`. The biome: `document.documentElement.dataset.biome`; `import('/src/ui/Flavor.ts')` for `setBiome`, `fl`, `FLAVOR`.
  - Bench a map through the real path (open a mode's maps screen first): a match started straight from the stage with `__dev.start` reads ~1 ms high on the jungle for ~30 s (`docs/BENCH-16.0.json`).
- Thermals (17.0):
  - `await __dev.vramCycle({ laps: 3, play })`: Neon Block → Crash Site → the main menu (the stage) → Neon Docks, every switch through the real UI (the result screen's cards and MAIN MENU, the menu's mode, map and DEPLOY), `laps` times. At each stop: `renderer.info.memory` (geometries, textures), compiled programs, scene objects and the point lights the renderer draws. `ok` compares the last lap with the one before (the first lap also compiles variants first met in play). GC never frees GPU memory: a growing count is an undisposed resource.
  - `__game.lightPool.debug()`: the level's point lights (`sources`), the real lights standing in for them (`slots`, the preset's budget or fewer) and how many are lit now.
  - `node scripts/csscheck.mjs [seconds=5]` (Node 22+, Google Chrome; `URL=` for another server): with the game's loop stopped, the browser's own main-thread work per second on the title and on the pause menu (CSS animations, style recalcs, layouts). It is headless (SwiftShader), so compositing cost doesn't show; in real Chrome sample the GPU and renderer processes' CPU time (`ps -o time`) over 10 s with the loop stopped (`__game.renderer.renderer.setAnimationLoop(null)`).
  - `scripts/lib/cdp.mjs` now kills its headless Chrome on every exit path (an exception, Ctrl-C): a crashed leak check once left one rendering for hours.
- Stairs (9.1): `__dev.world()` also reports a stair flush with a wall (`stair flush with wall`); `await __dev.stairGaps()` lists every stair-to-wall gap (3 cm) and presses a body into each one to check it keeps its floor (`drops: 0`).
- Music (9.0): `__game.debug().music` (current track, file or procedural, live sources, files found); `await __dev.musicSoak({ cycles: 3 })` cycles every level and the title and checks that every music source is disposed (`unaccounted: 0`).
- Arena bots (7.0), on the desert. Every TDM check takes `ai: 'arena'` (the default) or `ai: 'tactical'` (the 6.0 squads):
  - `await __dev.tdmSim({ ai: 'arena', tickets: 25 })` plays a whole match headless, bot vs bot. It steps a fixed 1/60 s with no rendering, as fast as the CPU allows, so the result doesn't depend on the window's frame rate. It reports:
    - match length, kills per minute, kill ranges;
    - how much the bots move, crouch and stand still;
    - jumps, vaults, pushes and push kills, BREAK share, target switches (and why);
    - sight-to-first-shot times, stuck bots, friendly fire;
    - AI ms per step and the sensing counters.
  - `await __dev.tdmAB({ runs: 3 })`: `tdmSim` for both AIs, alternating, with the medians side by side. Compare with `docs/BENCH-7.0.json`.
  - `TDM.ai = 'tactical'` (in `src/levels/Tdm.ts`) brings back the 6.0 bots in play.
- Neon Docks (8.0): every TDM check runs on the loaded level if it has an arena (`?setting=docks`). `tdmSim` also reports time and kills per zone and the share of time off the floor, and takes `roster: 'any'` (blades back) and `laneLeave: Infinity` (lane discipline off) for A/Bs. `bench(3)` has a docks set: seven views and a firefight, with the match running.

## Source layout

- `src/core`: game loop and states (title, intro, play, paused, dead, ending), renderer and post chain, input
- `src/stealth`:
  - `Perception`: the shared `VISION` config and maths;
  - alert state machine;
  - Guard AI;
  - Security: dispatch, reinforcements, search roles, hearing;
  - `PlayerIntel`: what the player knows, for the minimap.
- `src/core/Interaction`: the single E handler (tap and hold)
- `src/city`: instanced civilian crowd (witnesses, reports, panic)
- `src/story`:
  - `Mission`: the authoritative state (acts, steps, checkpoints, set pieces);
  - `Objectives`: what each step means, where, and hints;
  - `StoryPresenter`: banner, panel, marker, TAB overview, hints, tips, subtitles;
  - `Guidance`: routes on the nav graph;
  - the ending.
- `src/cinematic`: the story opening (`StoryIntro`) and the arcade intro
- `src/core/Performance`, `Bindings`: frame pacing, presets, key bindings
- `src/world/Manhunt`: wanted billboard and posters
- `src/rail`: timetable (unit-tested), rail controller, metro train with walk-in interior
- `src/world`:
  - `District`: layout and nav
  - `Architecture`: walkways, stairs, railings, rooftops
  - `Metro`: the station
  - `City`, `PropKit`, `Fountain`, `Train`, `Events`, `Zones`
  - collision: boxes, ramps, cylinders, holes and vault flags, with a grid broadphase
- `src/gameplay`: cabinets, medkits, breakable lights
- `src/audio`: Web Audio SFX and ambience with volume groups; `music/` holds the MusicManager (named tracks, crossfades, drop-in files), the Tokyo score and the pattern themes
- `src/levels`: the level contract and registry, arcade rules, and one folder per setting (`tokyo`, `desert`, `jungle`, `docks`)
- `src/entities`: weapon specs and enemy looks per setting (`weapons/`, `enemies/`)
- `src/player`: FPS controller and the viewmodel weapon (holster, recoil, reload, pump and tube-loading mechanics)
- `src/enemies`: models (merged per joint), Runner, Gunner (two-rate tactical brains), nav and arcade waves
- `src/enemies/tactics`: the squad director, time-slicing scheduler, utility selection, dynamic cover and generated cover spots, callouts
- `src/levels/docks` (8.0): Neon Docks, the TDM arena (plan, lanes and zones in `DocksLayout`)
- `src/enemies/Roster.ts` (8.0): roster filtering (a mode requests unit classes)
- `src/enemies/arena` (7.0, Team Deathmatch only): the deathmatch brain, the arena director (fronts, momentum, contact board, push claims), attention-driven sensing, jump and vault movement, the arena bodies
- `src/ui`: HUD, story HUD, minimap, marker, pause and settings menu, end screen
- `src/dev`: route check and console helpers (DEV only)
